**Full description** (4,000 characters max):

```
WillSpot keeps your Naira and your crypto in one simple wallet, built for Nigeria.

ADD MONEY YOUR WAY
Get your own account number and fund your wallet with a normal bank transfer. Your balance updates as soon as the money lands.

BUY AND SELL CRYPTO AT A LOCKED PRICE
Buy or sell USDT, Bitcoin and Ethereum with Naira. Every quote shows the rate, what you pay and what you get, and the price is held while you confirm. No hidden fees: the rate already includes our margin.

CONVERT AND CASH OUT
Swap between Naira, dollars and crypto, and withdraw to any Nigerian bank account. Save the accounts you use most.

PAY BILLS AND TRADE GIFT CARDS
Buy airtime and data, pay for electricity and TV, and sell or buy gift cards, all from the same balance.

EVERY KOBO ACCOUNTED FOR
A clear history with receipts for every transaction, plus monthly statements you can download as a PDF and share.

PROTECTED BY YOUR PIN
- Every payment, trade and withdrawal needs your transaction PIN.
- The app locks itself when you step away, and hides your balances in the app switcher.
- Turn on two-step sign-in with an authenticator app.
- See every device signed in to your account, and sign out the ones you don't recognise.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Quick answers in the help centre, and a support team you can message from inside the app. We will never ask for your PIN, password or codes.

We verify your identity (BVN, and a selfie for higher limits) before you can add money or trade.
```

## Claims to avoid until they're true and checked by counsel

- "Licensed", "regulated", "approved by the SEC/CBN", "insured" or "NDIC-protected": none of these is established yet (RESEARCH, counsel questions).
- "Instant" withdrawals: payout speed depends on the bank provider.
- "Free" or "zero fees": the rate includes a margin, and withdrawals may carry a fee.
- "Bank-grade security" or "100% secure": unmeasurable, and Apple reviewers reject it.
- Language support beyond what is reviewed (see docs/TRANSLATIONS.md).
